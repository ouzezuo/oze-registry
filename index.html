About This Terminal

OZE Registry Office — Official Registry of Divergent Occurrences

Mandate

The OZE Registry Office is the designated institutional body for the identification, cataloging, and archival management of divergent occurrences across documented worlds and eras.

Public Access Terminal

This terminal provides restricted public access to selected registry records. Redacted, sealed, corrupted, or internally disputed materials may appear as partial entries.

Classification System

Records are organized under four primary layers: Entities (ENT), Case Dossiers (DOS-CASE), Internal Case Items (CASE-[ID]-[TYPE]), and Global Historical Events (EVT). Chronology is recorded relative to The Exodus (2468). 2026 corresponds to 442 BX.
