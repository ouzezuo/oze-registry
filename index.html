About This Terminal

Mandate

The OZE Registry Office is the designated institutional body for the identification, cataloging, and archival management of divergent occurrences across documented worlds and eras. The registry operates under the chronological framework established by The Exodus (EVT-CAT-2345).

Public Access Terminal

This terminal provides restricted public access to selected registry records. Materials available through this interface represent a curated subset of the complete institutional archive. The terminal is not a complete record.
